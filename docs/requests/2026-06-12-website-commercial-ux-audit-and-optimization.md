# Request: Website Audit, Login Reliability, and Commercial-Grade UX Refinement

**Date:** 2026-06-12  
**Requester:** Coach / Athlete (User)  
**Objective:** Meticulously audit and optimize the AquaFlow Pro application to ensure it reaches a commercial-grade user experience and absolute technical reliability, resolving login issues, data sync/transmission limits, dashboard functionality, and athlete interaction features.

## 1. Scope of Work

### 1. System Access and Infrastructure
- **(a) Reliable Login and General Usability:** Check login flows for both coach and athlete, ensuring credentials can log in reliably from any device. Ensure error handling is friendly, error feedback is clear, and user redirection is seamless.
- **(b) Data Transmission / Sync:** Audit data transmission between the server/API and the frontend. Ensure payload size constraints, connection timeouts, or Edge runtime limitations are handled robustly without dropping connections or failing to load pages.

### 2. Coaching Dashboard
- **(a) Training Session Publishing:** Ensure training sessions publish reliably, error validation is robust, and the layout for plan editing is correct.
- **(b) Check-in/Attendance Tracking:** Audit check-in functionality, verifying attendance statistics, records, and history.

### 3. Athlete Interface
- **(a) XP and Playability:** Audit the gamification elements (e.g., XP tracking, levels, avatar shop/inventory, buddy system) to ensure they work smoothly.
- **(b) Coach Messages / Announcements:** Ensure communication flow from coach dashboard (announcements, feedback responses) is received instantly and reliably on the athlete side.
- **(c) Login and Access:** Confirm that the athlete login is bulletproof and doesn't lead to white screens, crash loops, or cookie issues.
- **(d) Performance and Health Reporting:** Verify performance metrics (best times, results) and health reporting (body injury map, pain scale, uploads, and physical condition tracking).
- **(e) Training Feedback:** Verify feedback forms, saving drafts, and uploading training feedback photographs.
